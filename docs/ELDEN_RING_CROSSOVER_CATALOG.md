# Elden Ring Crossover Catalog

This is a compact Game Boy crossover set, not a one-to-one dump of every item
from Elden Ring. The current game has one weapon, armor, off-hand, and trinket
slot, a 16-item bag, and a 24-item stash, so armor sets are represented as one
equippable piece and weapons use the existing four-slot model.

## Nine loot ranks

The item stores a zero-based rarity byte, while menus and full names show ranks
1–9. Higher ranks increase base stats, affix count, and sale value.

| Rank | Rarity title | Stat multiplier | Rolled affixes |
|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | Worn | 0.875× | 0 |
| 2 | Sturdy | 1.0× | 0 |
| 3 | Fine | 1.125× | 1 |
| 4 | Enchanted | 1.25× | 1 |
| 5 | Rare | 1.5× | 2 |
| 6 | Epic | 1.75× | 2 |
| 7 | Legend | 2.125× | 3 |
| 8 | Relic | 2.5× | 3 |
| 9 | Mythic | 3.0× | 3 |

Boss drops receive a strong rarity bonus. The inventory and shop use the same
rank labels and rarity colors.

## Tower bosses

Every fifth tower floor now cycles through this nine-boss title roster. The
rank in the title is the boss progression rank; it is separate from loot rank.
The existing monster sprites and S-tier boss combat stats provide the visual
and mechanical stand-ins.

| Rank | Boss title | Existing sprite stand-in |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Margit, the Fell Omen | Death Knight |
| 2 | Godrick the Grafted | Bugbear |
| 3 | Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon | Mind Flayer |
| 4 | Starscourge Radahn | Dragon |
| 5 | Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy | Dragon |
| 6 | Morgott, the Omen King | Death Knight |
| 7 | Malenia, Blade of Miquella | Displacer Beast |
| 8 | Maliketh, the Black Blade | Death Knight |
| 9 | Radagon and the Elden Beast | Beholder |

After rank 9, the tower repeats the roster at stronger tower levels. The
existing potion, ether, remedy, elixir, regeneration, haste, and buff items
remain in the quick-use inventory.
