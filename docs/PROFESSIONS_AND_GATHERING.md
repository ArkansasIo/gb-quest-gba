# Professions and Gathering

The pause menu's **PROF** page tracks 18 professions from level 1 to 100.
Six gathering professions collect materials from field nodes; twelve crafting
professions spend those materials on gear or consumables. Profession levels,
experience, and materials are saved with the character. Version-2 cartridge
saves migrate to the new save format with professions initialized at level 1.

## Gathering

Each non-town world zone has one visible field node. Face it and press A to
gather. A node can be harvested once during that visit; returning to a zone
regenerates its node. The zone's biome selects two possible gathering tracks:

| Biome | Gathering tracks |
|---|---|
| Forest | Herbalism, Logging |
| Plains | Logging, Fishing |
| Desert | Mining, Archaeology |
| Swamp | Herbalism, Fishing |
| Foothills | Mining, Skinning |
| Coast | Fishing, Skinning |
| Snowfield | Skinning, Logging |
| Volcanic | Mining, Archaeology |
| Underground | Mining, Archaeology |

Gathering yields Ore, Herbs, Wood, Hide, Fish, or Relics. Higher profession
levels can yield extra materials. Each successful gather grants profession XP.
Materials stack to 99.

## Crafting

Open the PROF page and switch to Crafting with LEFT or RIGHT. Crafting requires
a town; select a recipe and press A. Gear goes to the bag, so leave a free slot
before crafting. Crafted gear's item level follows the current area's level,
and profession rank raises its minimum rarity.

| Crafting profession | Recipe | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Blacksmithing | 4 Ore + 1 Wood | Armor |
| Weaponsmithing | 4 Ore + 2 Wood | Weapon |
| Armorsmithing | 3 Ore + 3 Hide | Armor |
| Leatherworking | 4 Hide + 1 Herb | Armor |
| Tailoring | 2 Hide + 3 Herbs | Armor |
| Jewelcrafting | 2 Ore + 2 Relics | Trinket |
| Alchemy | 3 Herbs + 1 Fish | Elixir |
| Enchanting | 2 Relics + 2 Herbs | Rare or better gear |
| Engineering | 3 Ore + 3 Wood | Off-hand |
| Cooking | 2 Fish + 2 Herbs | Regeneration tonic |
| Runecrafting | 3 Relics + 1 Ore | Trinket |
| Inscription | 2 Herbs + 2 Wood | Gear |

The separate town forge upgrades already-equipped items with gold. Blacksmithing,
Weaponsmithing, and Armorsmithing create new equipment using gathered resources.
